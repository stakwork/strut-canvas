# Call Summary

When a recording stops, the `summarize` workflow writes the call's
summary from its transcript, and the canvas's Calls page shows it. This
page is how to write one.

- **Title**: what the call was about, under 80 characters, with no quotes
  and no date. Name its two or three subjects ("Release process, disk
  alerts, the new demo box"), never "Team call".
- **Discussed**: three to eight points, each one concrete thing. Name the
  people, products, features, pull requests and numbers that were said,
  not themes.
- **Decided**: only what was agreed or chosen, a priority that was set
  among them. An idea somebody floated is not a decision.
- **Action items**: one entry per person, by first name, with what they
  said they would do or were asked to do on the call. Jamie's only when a
  task was clearly given to Jamie.
- **Open**: questions nobody answered, blockers, things left unfinished.

Keep each point to one sentence, and call people by their first names.
The transcript is noisy: a name or a product word may be misheard, so
spell it the way most of the call spells it. A call with nothing of
substance gets one Discussed point that says so, and the other lists stay
empty.
